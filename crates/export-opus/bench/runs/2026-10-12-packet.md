# 2026-10-12 — one-packet bench

`cargo bench --workspace --bench packet` from `crates/export-opus`.

Threading off. Clock is `std::time::Instant` (not a cycle counter). Nearest-rank p50 and p99 over 20,000 calls after 1,000 warmup calls. No speedup is claimed.

The three timed calls are encode of 960 silence samples, decode of that packet, and decode of an empty packet (`Error::Truncated`).

## Runs on this machine

Same binary profile (`opt-level = 3`, `lto = "thin"`, `codegen-units = 1`). The code-3 parser is not on these three paths. The spread is timer noise.

| run | encode p50 | encode p99 | decode p50 | decode p99 | truncated p50 | truncated p99 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 195 ns | 346 ns | 45 ns | 61 ns | 25 ns | 26 ns |
| 2 | 206 ns | 239 ns | 51 ns | 59 ns | 27 ns | 29 ns |
| 3 (this tree) | 350 ns | 521 ns | 68 ns | 85 ns | 36 ns | 42 ns |

Encode p99 moved from 239 ns to 521 ns across runs. Treat that table as an observation, not a stable figure.

## Not a latency claim

These calls are a few dozen nanoseconds of work under a clock that also moved by hundreds of nanoseconds. A later run that differs by less than that spread does not show a regression or a win.
